# Message to Mom (draft)

> From docs/13. Send it as a message so the "yes" is in writing. Edit the tone to how you two talk.

## English

Mom, I want to build a small tool for your shop's Shopee and TikTok messages, as a project for my job search. It would put both inboxes in one list, sort the urgent ones to the top, look up the order, and suggest a reply in the buyer's language. **You still read and send every reply yourself, and you still decide all refunds.**

Can you say yes or no to these?

1. I can see buyer messages and orders from your shop, only to build and test this tool.
2. Buyer names, phone numbers and addresses are not stored or shown anywhere.
3. I can show results in my portfolio only with everything anonymized (no shop name, no buyer details).
4. The code is mine; the shop's data stays yours, and I delete my copy if you ask.

And a few things I need from you, when you have time:

- **Logins:** I never need your password. Later you log in yourself on Shopee and TikTok to approve my app.
- **Who replies:** does anyone else answer buyers besides you?
- **Rules:** your return, refund, exchange, cancel and delivery rules, and how Shopee and TikTok differ.
- **Your replies:** your saved quick replies, and anything the tool must never say or promise.
- **Examples:** 100–200 past chats from both platforms, including the difficult ones (screenshots are fine).
- **Orders:** your SiteGiant plan, and whether it can export orders (one sample export is enough).
- **Volume:** roughly how many messages a day on each platform, busy hours, and the language mix.
- **Time:** can I time you for one week while you answer messages, and have 30 minutes each week for feedback?

## 中文

妈，我想为你店里的 Shopee 和 TikTok 买家消息做一个小工具，也是我找工作用的作品。它会把两个平台的消息放在同一个列表里，把急的排在最前面，自动找到对应订单，并用买家的语言建议一个回复。**每一条回复还是由你自己看过、自己发送，退款也全部由你决定。**

请你回复同意或不同意：

1. 我可以看店里的买家消息和订单，只用来开发和测试这个工具。
2. 买家的名字、电话和地址不会被保存或展示。
3. 我可以把结果放进作品集，但全部匿名（不出现店名和买家资料）。
4. 代码归我；店里的数据归你，你要求的话我会删除我的副本。

另外，等你有空时需要这些：

- **登录：** 我不需要你的密码。之后你自己登录 Shopee 和 TikTok 批准我的应用就可以。
- **谁回复：** 除了你，还有其他人回复买家吗？
- **规则：** 退货、退款、换货、取消和发货/送达的规则，以及 Shopee 和 TikTok 有什么不同。
- **你的回复：** 你保存的快捷回复，以及工具绝对不能说或承诺的事情。
- **例子：** 两个平台过去的 100–200 段对话，包括难处理的（截图也可以）。
- **订单：** 你用的 SiteGiant 方案，能不能导出订单（一份样本就够）。
- **数量：** 每个平台每天大概多少条消息、什么时候最忙、买家用什么语言。
- **时间：** 可以让我在你回消息时计时一个星期吗？以后每周给我 30 分钟反馈。
