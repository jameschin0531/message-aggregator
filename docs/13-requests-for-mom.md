# 13 What to ask Mom for

> Keep it short and concrete. Do not ask for passwords.

## Agreement

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| A clear yes in writing (a message is fine): what I may see, how it is used, whether anonymized results can go in my portfolio, who owns the code | Avoids misunderstandings; basis for privacy |
| Who else (staff, family) replies to buyers | Priority rules and the baseline |

## Authorization and accounts

| Item | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Her shop region, shop type and shop ID on each platform | Check API eligibility | |
| Her logging in herself to Shopee Seller Centre and TikTok Shop Seller Center to approve my app | Official OAuth flow | I never need her password and should not log in as her |
| Any documents a platform asks for during review (for example business registration) | Some reviews need them [Unverified] | She sends them to the platform directly; I do not keep copies |
| Her SiteGiant plan, and whether it can export orders, products and customers, or has an API | Order data source | |

## Business knowledge

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Return, refund, exchange, cancellation, shipping and delivery rules; differences between Shopee and TikTok | Policy table |
| Her saved quick replies and how she usually writes | Draft style |
| Product list, size charts, common questions | Draft content |
| What the system must never say or promise | Safety rules |

## Data

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| 100 to 200 past conversations from both platforms, including hard ones | Test set |
| A sample order export | Order matching and the data model |
| Messages per day per platform, busy hours, language mix | Cost and evaluation |

## How she works

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Who replies, and when | Baseline and priority rules |
| How she decides a refund; when she steps in herself | Escalation rules |
| What annoys her most about messages today | Focus on real pain |

## Time

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Permission to time her for a week | Baseline |
| 30 minutes of feedback each week | Real-use feedback and fixes |
| Agreement on what "good enough" looks like | Success criteria |
